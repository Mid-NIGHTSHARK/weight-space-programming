The policy moves the paddle toward a one-step projection of the ball’s horizontal motion: `2 × ball_x − trail_x`. The first-layer units encode the ball, trail, and paddle columns. Their small input weights keep `tanh` close to linear; the second-layer unit combines them into projected-target minus paddle position, and its `tanh` gives the movement direction. The output logits favor left or right according to that sign, favor no-op when aligned, and suppress actions that do nothing.
